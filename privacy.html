Privacy policy

genealogy-backup · effective 20 October 2026

What the app accesses

Only files in the owner's own Google Drive that the app itself created (Google Drive scope drive.file). The app is used solely by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

How the data is used

Files are copied between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive, and checked against their local copies. Nothing else is done with them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, keep on any server, share, transfer or sell any user data.
  • It does not use data for advertising, analytics or profiling, and has no third-party services.
  • It does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
