genealogy-backup

genealogy-backup is a private backup tool used by one person — the owner of this site. It runs rclone with the owner's own Google OAuth client to copy the owner's own files (personal documents, scans and photos) from the owner's computers to the owner's own Google Drive and to read them back.

The tool is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.
